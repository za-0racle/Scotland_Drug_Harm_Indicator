# Archived PHS RADAR alerts

Source: Public Health Scotland, FOI response PHS FOI 2026-002533, and three supplied RADAR alert PDFs. The response covers topics first issued from 1 July 2022 through 31 December 2024 and provides later version/status history. It states that none of the alerts was withdrawn during that period; the bromazolam and xylazine alerts were subsequently archived and replaced by broader alerts in February 2026.

Included PDFs are the supplied 14 July 2026 publications: alerts 2384 (nitazenes, v2.0), 2385 (new benzodiazepines, v2.0), and 2386 (xylazine-type drugs/medetomidine, v2.0), plus the FOI response letter. The letter lists nitazenes v2.1 on 27 October 2026; that later publication copy was not among the supplied PDFs. `version-history.csv` records the timeline described in the response letter.

These are retrospective publications, not live warnings. The alert narratives are contextual source material; they are not a validated quantitative series or evidence that dashboard flags detected an alert.
