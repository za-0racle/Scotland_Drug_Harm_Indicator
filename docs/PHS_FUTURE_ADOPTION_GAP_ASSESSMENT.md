# Future institutional evaluation and adoption gap assessment

Assessment date: 10 November 2026. This is a research-team engineering/scientific assessment, not a PHS assessment, agreement, endorsement or deployment approval. No institutional acceptance is implied. The prototype uses reviewed aggregate data and frozen analytical artifacts; clinical or operational warning performance has not been established.

## Accessibility reference and scope

W3C WCAG 2.2 specifies text-contrast requirements and keyboard/focus criteria; sampled colour checks and a skip-link test are only part of that scope. The current acceptance checks are not a conformance certificate. Review against the complete [W3C WCAG 2.2 Recommendation](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/), particularly [Contrast (Minimum)](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/contrast-minimum.html) and [Focus Visible](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/focus-visible.html). References reviewed on 10 November 2026.

## FOI and official communications

PHS publishes its own [protocol for handling information requests](https://publichealthscotland.scot/publications/public-health-scotland-protocol-for-handling-information-requests/public-health-scotland-protocol-for-handling-information-requests-version-6/). That official source is background on information requests; it does not supply the pending response or authorise this application. The research team must assess the actual response and disclosure/publication conditions when received. No official historical alert has been added, no validated reference-event set exists here, and no external warning metric was calculated.

## Recommended evaluation sequence

1. Demonstrate the local aggregate-data prototype using the Phase 8 workflow and invite scientific/data-governance feedback without claiming institutional approval.
2. Agree a bounded institutional evaluation scope and accountable owners; complete permissions, identity, hosting/security and accessibility assessments before access outside the approved local research environment.
3. Resolve publication-vintage/denominator and external-reference gaps; pre-specify independent scientific evaluation before tuning or warning-performance claims.
4. Treat any operational pilot as a separate decision requiring scientific, governance, security, reliability and human-response approval. An accurate retrospective dashboard alone does not satisfy these gates.

No work in this gap assessment was deployed, no permissions were changed, and no operational alert was activated.
